# Code of Conduct

EffectTrace adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, by reference:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

It applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews
and any other channel the project uses) and when an individual represents the
project in public.

## Summary

This summary does not replace the full text.

- Be respectful and welcoming. Assume good intent and accept that people have
  different experiences and viewpoints.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas and code,
  not people.
- Do not harass, insult, demean or threaten anyone, and do not use sexualized
  language or imagery.
- Do not publish others' private information without explicit permission.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the
repository's GitHub private reporting channel:
<https://github.com/effecttrace/effecttrace/security/advisories/new>.
State in the title that it is a Code of Conduct report. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainers ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) are responsible for
enforcement and follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines:
correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, depending on the impact
of the behaviour. A maintainer who is involved in a report does not take part
in handling it.
